What is MockMitra?

MockMitra is a free, AI-powered Computer Based Test (CBT) mock test platform built specifically for Indian competitive exam aspirants. It provides realistic exam simulations that mirror actual exam interfaces, helping students practice and prepare for government and competitive exams.

MockMitra uses artificial intelligence to generate exam-quality questions based on real exam blueprints and patterns. Students can also practice with Previous Year Question (PYQ) papers in a timed, exam-like environment.

⚠️ Important: MockMitra is a competitive exam mock test platform. It is NOT an interview preparation tool or mock interview platform. MockMitra is designed exclusively for CBT-based competitive exam practice.
